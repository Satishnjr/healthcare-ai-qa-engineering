# Product Vision

## Product Name
CareFlow Health

## Vision
CareFlow Health provides a production-like, frontend-only healthcare management experience for demonstrating advanced QA automation and AI-driven quality engineering workflows.

## Mission
Create a realistic healthcare management portal simulation that supports end-to-end requirement-to-testing traceability and serves as the system under test for Playwright, Cucumber, RAG, MCP, and LangGraph-based QA workflows.

## Problem Statement
Healthcare operations rely on fast, accurate workflows across reception, clinical staff, administrators, and patients. Teams need a realistic application context to validate UI behavior, role-based flows, error handling, and quality engineering automation without requiring production infrastructure or sensitive data.

## Target Users
- Front Desk / Receptionist: patient registration, search, and appointment coordination.
- Doctor: patient lookup, schedule review, clinical note updates, and care review.
- Nurse: patient support workflows and observation recording.
- Healthcare Administrator: operational oversight, user/doctor management, and reporting.
- Patient: self-service view for appointments, records, prescriptions, and notifications.

## Product Goals
- Simulate realistic healthcare operational workflows in a frontend-only architecture.
- Enable robust UI automation using BDD and Playwright.
- Provide structured requirement artifacts for traceability and AI-assisted QA workflows.
- Demonstrate enterprise-like engineering standards with reproducible implementation steps.
- Maintain security/compliance posture using synthetic data only.

## Non-Goals
- No real patient data
- No real healthcare integrations
- No real insurance provider integration
- No real payment gateway
- No real hospital integration
- No backend
- No API
- No production PHI

## Success Criteria
- Requirements hierarchy is clearly defined from product vision through automation.
- Product modules and user journeys are documented and internally consistent.
- QA strategy is documented with frontend-only constraints.
- AI/RAG relevance and traceability chain are defined for future implementation.
- Step artifacts are complete, auditable, and reproducible.
